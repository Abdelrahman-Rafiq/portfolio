Featured Project

Smart Office Occupancy Detection

Classification • Business-driven model evaluation

Predicts whether a meeting room is occupied or empty from environmental sensor data (temperature, humidity, light, CO₂, humidity ratio), so lighting and HVAC can switch off automatically and save energy.

  • The challenge A smart-building client required at least 97% accuracy; below that, lights would switch off on people in meetings and the system would be removed.
  • The approach Data audit and EDA, then five models compared against a majority-class baseline with scaled pipelines and stratified 5-fold CV. The best model (KNN) was tuned with grid search and checked with time-block validation and permutation importance.
  • The result About 99.4% test accuracy against the 97% target. Errors were analysed by business cost: wrongly “empty” (lights off on people) matters far more than wrongly “occupied” (wasted energy).
