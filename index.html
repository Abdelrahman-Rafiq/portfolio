About Me

When I first started in Computer Science, I realized quickly that frameworks come and go, but the underlying computation never changes. I wanted to know what happens beneath the abstractions.

That curiosity led me to C, Harvard's CS50, and data structures—a path that earned me a HackerRank Gold Medal in C. But theory only matters when put to work. As a control systems engineer on our Robo-Tech team, I took that low-level mindset to the water, writing sensor-fusion and PID algorithms under pressure to help us earn 4th Place at the MATE 2026 Regional

Today, I'm channeling that same systems-first mindset into Data Science. Through the DEPI Data Science track and AWS Academy (Cloud & ML Foundations certified), I'm pairing rigorous mathematics—linear algebra, probability, and calculus—with practical machine learning pipelines.

I don't just chase tools; I build the foundational depth that makes any tool easier to master—delivering data solutions built to last long after current trends fade.
